About me

How do you start a new project?

I start by listening. Every project begins with a short discovery phase where I learn about your goals, challenges, and expectations. From there, I define the creative direction, scope, and deliverables, and set a timeline that balances clarity with craft.

Can you help with strategy, not just design?

Yes — strategy is part of everything I do. Before I design, I help define your message, audience, and positioning. That foundation shapes every decision that follows.

Do you also build what you design?

Yes. As a design engineer I take work from concept to production-ready code — components, interactions, and front-end architecture — so nothing gets lost in handoff.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on the scope and level of detail. A small website or product refresh may take around 4–6 weeks, while larger, more complex projects can run 2–4 months.
